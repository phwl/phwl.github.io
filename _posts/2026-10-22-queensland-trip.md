# Fishing
Unfortunately, a nearby cyclone prevented us from going offshore. The first day we went crabbing in the small boat.

{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/2026/10/IMG_6535.jpeg" caption="" %}

This wooden boat has had better days.

{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/2026/10/IMG_6560.jpeg" caption="" %}

{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/2026/10/IMG_6564.jpg" caption="" %}

There is nothing better than Cantonese style mud crab with ginger and shallots.

{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/2026/10/IMG_6612.jpeg" caption="" %}

The next day we went a few kilometers offshore in the bigger boat. We had excellent fishing, hooking about half a dozen good fish. Sadly, we lost them all.

{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/2026/10/IMG_6593.jpeg" caption="" %}

{% include figure image_path="/assets/images/2026/10/IMG_6602.jpg" caption="" %}

The first broke my 30lb rod. That's two travel rods broken in 3 trips. I don't think I'll do bottom fishing with travel rods any more. The other fish were all lost to sharks.

# Knife making
Next we made a knife from a leaf spring. Kwan made an amazing forge that uses
sump oil as fuel.
